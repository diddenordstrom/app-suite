# Fartlek starts the block after a 10 km+ race with recovery weeks gated by days since the race

Settled with the user on 2026-11-24, as a spec for a build session. It closes the recovery gap left open in [race blocks](fartlek-race-blocks.md) ("Recovery after the race waits"), named as the biggest gap in [race-specific phase](fartlek-race-specific-phase.md) (todo 1) and open in [goal-driven blocks](fartlek-block-goals.md). The [build plan](#build-plan) below is written so the work can be done without re-deriving anything; nothing of it is built yet.

## Decided

- **Scope: races of 10 km and longer.** Recovery applies after an **A or B** race of **10 km or more** (10K, half, marathon). Below 10 km there are no recovery weeks; the race week's existing cap (`aroundRace`: only short easy runs after the race) is enough. **C races get no recovery.** The app mainly targets the marathon and half.
- **Distance buckets, not interpolation.** `>= 10` and `< 15` km reads the 10K row, `>= 15` and `< 30` the half row, `>= 30` the marathon row. Interpolating between rows would invent values the sources don't give.
- **No block is generated by itself.** The race stays at the end of its 2:2 form-peak block as today. When the runner creates the **next** block ("Skapa block") and such a race was run recently, that block's first week or weeks get a new week role, **`recovery`**. After them the block builds as usual, and the existing re-entry (`BLOCK.reentryGrowth` up to the tolerated peak) brings volume back toward the old peak.
- **The 14-day refusal goes.** `BLOCK.afterRaceDays` and `raceJustRun` in [`routes/blockplan.ts`](../../apps/fartlek/src/server/routes/blockplan.ts) are removed; the recovery weeks replace them. A re-plan's weeks after a race inside it (`replanRace`, today filled with `lighter`) get recovery weeks instead.
- **Gated in days since the race, not weeks.** Day 0 is race day. A block created 10 days after the race counts those 10 days as done: a recovery week that is already over is dropped, and the quality gates below open on the same calendar day whenever the block starts.
- **The volume reference is the pre-race peak build week**, the same reference the taper uses (`volumes()` takes taper and race weeks as a share of the biggest build week).
- **The rule table (A race).** Volume as a share of the pre-race peak week, one share per recovery week. Days are days since the race; a session type is allowed **from** that day on.

  | Row | Recovery weeks | Volume per week | No running | Strides from | Threshold (short) from | Threshold (full) from | Intervals from |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 10K (10–15 km) | 1 | 70 % | – | day 4 | day 7 | day 7 | day 10 |
  | Half (15–30 km) | 2 | 50 % · 75 % | – | day 6 | day 11 | day 11 | day 14 |
  | Marathon (30 km+) | 4 | 15 % · 45 % · 65 % · 80 % | days 1–3 (walk or cross-train) | day 12 | day 21 | day 28 | day 28 |

  Where the sources give a range the table picks one value: half threshold day 10–12 → 11, marathon strides day 10–14 → 12, marathon intervals day 28–35 → 28, marathon rest 2–4 days → 3. The long run is capped low in recovery weeks (see `RECOVERY.longShare` and `longMaxKm`).
- **A B race is about 0.6 of an A race.** Number of weeks and every day gate × 0.6, rounded to the nearest whole number (weeks at least 1); the volume shares are the last weeks of the A row, each moved 40 % closer to 100 % and rounded to 5 % (so they start higher). Written out as literal rows in the table below so nothing is computed at run time.

  | Row (B) | Weeks | Volume | No running | Strides | Threshold short / full | Intervals |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 10K | 1 | 80 % | – | day 2 | day 4 / 4 | day 6 |
  | Half | 1 | 85 % | – | day 4 | day 7 / 7 | day 8 |
  | Marathon | 2 | 80 % · 90 % | days 1–2 | day 7 | day 13 / 17 | day 17 |

- **The role is independent of where it sits.** `recovery` means "a week after a race, sized and gated from that race", not "the first week of a block". A future plan that spans several blocks (an 8–28 week marathon plan that ends with its recovery) can put recovery weeks anywhere and reuse the same role, table and gates.
- **Recovery weeks are never fitted upward, deepened or ramped.** They are a fixed share of the reference, outside the build-week ramp, the goal scaling, `fitWeeks`, the race phase's deep aim (`deepen`) and the taper search. They don't count as build weeks in the block's verdict (`evaluateBlock` judges build weeks only).
- **Not in `TUNE`.** The table is an injury guard (when the legs may run fast again), so like the spike rule and the form floor it stays a constant and is not in "Utvecklarinställningar" (see the rule at the top of [`shared/tuning.ts`](../../apps/fartlek/src/shared/tuning.ts)).

## Why

- **Distance decides the length.** The rules of thumb scale with the race: Pfitzinger's "one easy day per mile raced" and Daniels' "one easy day per 3 km raced" both give about a week after 10 km, two to three after a half and three to four after a marathon. The table sits inside those ranges.
- **The marathon damages muscle for weeks.** Biopsies after a marathon (Hikida 1983, Warhol 1985) show fibre damage and inflammation peaking in the first days to a week and repair lasting three to four weeks. Easy running from about 48 hours does not slow that repair, which is why the marathon row walks or cross-trains for three days and then runs easy, rather than resting for weeks.
- **Illness risk is raised for one to two weeks** after a marathon (Nieman's studies of marathon runners): another reason the first weeks stay small.
- **Order of return: volume, then strides, then threshold, then intervals.** Coaches agree on the order more than on the days; the gates encode the order with one day each.
- **Caveat.** The numbers are coarse. Several come from secondhand summaries of the books and studies, not the full texts, and none were tested on the engine's histories yet. Tune them with `npm run scenarios -w apps/fartlek` once built.

## Passed over

- **Generating a recovery block automatically after the form-peak block.** The runner creates one block at a time and sees it before it starts ([race blocks](fartlek-race-blocks.md): "blocks you can follow up, not one long plan"); a block that appears by itself breaks that.
- **Keeping the 14-day refusal next to the recovery weeks.** A refusal says nothing about what to run; the recovery weeks do, and they are shorter or longer by distance.
- **Gating in weeks.** A block created late would repeat recovery the runner has already done, and a race on a Saturday versus a Sunday would shift the gates by a week.
- **Interpolating by distance** (a 25 km race between the half and the marathon rows): no source gives the in-between values.

## Open

- **The result and how the race felt** (a slow finish, cramps, a DNF) could lengthen or shorten recovery. Parked for v1; the race result is already stored ([race results](fartlek-race-results.md)).
- **Modifiers for heat, a hilly course and age.** Parked for v1.
- **A B race in the middle of a block** keeps today's behaviour: the block ends at the B race week (`racePlanFor`), and the next block gets the shorter B recovery. A B race in the weeks of a re-plan gets the B rows through `replanRace`.
- ~~**Plans of several blocks (8–28 weeks).**~~ Decided 2026-11-24 in [race plan skeleton](fartlek-race-plan-skeleton.md): a skeleton to the race and through these recovery weeks, reusing the role and the table; this page's build plan comes first.
- **The long run's way back.** After the recovery weeks the long-run ladder steps up from the longest run planned in them, about 8 % a week, which may be slower than the weekly volume's re-entry. Check in the scenarios before changing it.
- **The threshold ladder after recovery** resumes at the rung the last threshold session left (`seed.rung`); whether it should step back a rung or two after a marathon is untested.

1. Extend the role union (around `export type WeekRole`):

   Update the doc comment above it: "`recovery`: a week after an A or B race of 10 km or more, a share of the pre-race peak with quality gated by days since the race (docs/decisions/fartlek-post-race-recovery.md)".

2. Add the race the recovery follows, and the field on `BlockSeed` (next to `race`):

   ```ts
   /** An A or B race of 10 km+ that recovery weeks follow (docs/decisions/fartlek-post-race-recovery.md). */
   export interface RecoveryRace {
     date: ISODate;
     distanceKm: number;
     priority: "A" | "B";
     /** Run km of the biggest week in the 6 weeks before the race week; null = take it from the plan or the tolerance. */
     peakKm: number | null;
   }
   // in BlockSeed:
   /** The race the first weeks recover from, or a race inside the weeks whose days after it are gated. */

   ```

3. Add the table and its helpers after `BLOCK`. Remove `afterRaceDays` (and its comment) from `BLOCK`.

   ```ts
   export interface RecoveryRow {
     /** Recovery weeks, and the volume of each as a share of the pre-race peak week. `volume.length === weeks`. */
     weeks: number;
     volume: readonly number[];
     /** Days 1..restDays after the race: no running (0 = none). */
     restDays: number;
     /** First day (days since the race, race day 0) each kind is allowed. */
     strides: number;
     thresholdShort: number;   // the lighter week's session, NEXT.lighterThr (3 × 6 min)
     thresholdFull: number;    // the ladder's rung
     intervals: number;        // not generated today; kept so a future generator (and race-phase blueprints) honours it
   }
   export type RecoveryBucket = "10k" | "half" | "marathon";

   /** Post-race recovery (docs/decisions/fartlek-post-race-recovery.md). An injury guard: not in TUNE. */
   export const RECOVERY = {
     /** Races shorter than this get no recovery weeks. Buckets: < `halfFromKm` 10k, < `marathonFromKm` half, else marathon. */
     minKm: 10, halfFromKm: 15, marathonFromKm: 30,
     /** Long run of a recovery week: this share of the week's km, never over `longMaxKm`, never under NEXT.easyMinKm. */
     longShare: 0.3, longMaxKm: 16,
     /** How far back a race can be and still give recovery weeks (the longest row's weeks). */
     lookbackDays: 28,
     A: {
       "10k":      { weeks: 1, volume: [0.7],                  restDays: 0, strides: 4,  thresholdShort: 7,  thresholdFull: 7,  intervals: 10 },
       half:       { weeks: 2, volume: [0.5, 0.75],            restDays: 0, strides: 6,  thresholdShort: 11, thresholdFull: 11, intervals: 14 },
       marathon:   { weeks: 4, volume: [0.15, 0.45, 0.65, 0.8], restDays: 3, strides: 12, thresholdShort: 21, thresholdFull: 28, intervals: 28 },
     },
     B: {
       "10k":      { weeks: 1, volume: [0.8],                  restDays: 0, strides: 2,  thresholdShort: 4,  thresholdFull: 4,  intervals: 6 },
       half:       { weeks: 1, volume: [0.85],                 restDays: 0, strides: 4,  thresholdShort: 7,  thresholdFull: 7,  intervals: 8 },
       marathon:   { weeks: 2, volume: [0.8, 0.9],             restDays: 2, strides: 7,  thresholdShort: 13, thresholdFull: 17, intervals: 17 },
     },
   } as const satisfies { A: Record<RecoveryBucket, RecoveryRow>; B: Record<RecoveryBucket, RecoveryRow> } & Record<string, unknown>;

   /** The bucket of a race distance; null under RECOVERY.minKm. */
   export const recoveryBucket = (km: number): RecoveryBucket | null =>
     km < RECOVERY.minKm ? null : km < RECOVERY.halfFromKm ? "10k" : km < RECOVERY.marathonFromKm ? "half" : "marathon";

   /** The row for a race; null for a C race or one under 10 km. */
   export function recoveryRow(r: Pick<RecoveryRace, "distanceKm"> & { priority: string }): RecoveryRow | null {
     const b = recoveryBucket(r.distanceKm);
     return b && (r.priority === "A" || r.priority === "B") ? RECOVERY[r.priority][b] : null;
   }

   /**
    * Which recovery week a plan week is: the one holding the week's middle day (weekStart + 3). Recovery week k covers
    * days 7k+1 .. 7k+7 after the race. Never negative (a week that holds the race reads 0).
    */
   export const recoveryWeekOf = (raceDate: ISODate, weekStart: ISODate) =>
     Math.max(0, Math.floor((daysBetween(raceDate, weekStart) + 2) / 7));

   /** "recovery" for each of `weeks` weeks from `startWeek` still inside the race's recovery, else null (left to the caller). */
   export function recoveryRoles(rec: RecoveryRace, startWeek: ISODate, weeks: number): (WeekRole | null)[] {
     const row = recoveryRow(rec);
     return Array.from({ length: weeks }, (_, i) => {
       const ws = addDays(startWeek, i * 7);
       return row && ws > rec.date && recoveryWeekOf(rec.date, ws) < row.weeks ? "recovery" : null;
     });
   }
   ```

   Notes for the implementer: `RECOVERY.A` and `.B` rows must satisfy `volume.length === weeks` (a test checks it). `as const satisfies` keeps the literal types; if TypeScript complains about the mixed record, drop the `& Record<string, unknown>` and type the two tables as separate constants `RECOVERY_A`, `RECOVERY_B`.

   Worked examples of `recoveryWeekOf` (put them in the test): race Sunday 2026-11-22, week from Monday 11-23 (day 1) → 0; Monday 11-30 (day 8) → 1. Race Saturday 11-21, Monday 11-23 (day 2) → 0. Race Wednesday 11-18, Monday 11-23 (day 5) → 1 (most of that week is in recovery week 1). Race Monday 11-16, Monday 11-23 (day 7) → 1.

### Step 2: volumes (`volumes()` in `shared/blockplan.ts`)

- `const lighter = role !== "build";` already makes a recovery week `lighter: true`; keep it (it keeps the tempo finish, cross minutes and `fitWeeks` off it).
- Add a branch **before** `else if (lighter)`:

  ```ts
  } else if (role === "recovery" && seed.recovery && recoveryRow(seed.recovery)) {
    const row = recoveryRow(seed.recovery)!;
    const k = Math.min(recoveryWeekOf(seed.recovery.date, addDays(seed.startWeek, i * 7)), row.volume.length - 1);
    // The pre-race peak: a build week planned before it (a re-plan with the race inside), else the run peak, else the tolerance.
    const plannedPeak = Math.max(0, ...out.filter((x) => x.role === "build").map((x) => x.km));
    const peak = plannedPeak || seed.recovery.peakKm || seed.tolerance.peakKm || Math.max(0, ...window.slice(-3));
    v = { km: fixed[i] ?? floorHalf(peak * row.volume[k]), cap: peak, reentry: false, lighter, role };
  }
  ```

  A `recovery` role without `seed.recovery` (the runner picked it in the editor with no race) falls through to the lighter branch: 70 % of the week before.
- `buildWeeks` counts only `isBuild`, so the ramp positions of the build weeks after the recovery weeks are unchanged. The first build week after recovery takes `blockWeekCap(window, tolerance)` with the recovery weeks in the window: re-entry (+30 % on the growth base, up to the tolerance) does the climb back. Recovery km are **not** held to `blockWeekCap`; they are under it by construction except right after the race week, where the cap would wrongly hold the marathon's 45 % week down.

### Step 3: sessions (`weeksFor()` in `shared/blockplan.ts`, `build()` in `shared/nextweek.ts`)

1. **Strides switch.** In `shared/nextweek.ts`, add `strides?: number` to `Shape` ("Easy runs with strides this week; default NEXT.stridesPerWeek") and in `build()` call `addStrides(items, seed.refs, shape.strides ?? NEXT.stridesPerWeek)`.
2. In `weeksFor`, compute once before the `vols.map`:

   ```ts
   const rec = seed.recovery ?? null;
   const row = rec ? recoveryRow(rec) : null;
   const dayOf = (d: ISODate) => (rec ? daysBetween(rec.date, d) : -1);
   let recLongest = 0, recStarted = false;
   ```

3. Inside the map, for each week (`ws.weekStart`, last day `addDays(ws.weekStart, 6)`):
   - **No race phase in a recovery week:** `const phase = seed.phase && v.role !== "race" && v.role !== "recovery" ? phaseWeekFor(...) : null;`
   - **Long run.** On the first recovery week set `longest = 0; any = 0; recStarted = true` before the long run is computed, so the weeks after step up from what recovery planned, not from the race or the long runs before it. In a recovery week:
     `longKm = Math.max(NEXT.easyMinKm, Math.min(RECOVERY.longMaxKm, floorHalf(v.km * RECOVERY.longShare)))`; `maxKm` stays `any > 0 ? spikeMax(any) : null`.
   - **Threshold.** When `row` and the week is a recovery week, or its first day is before `row.thresholdFull` (`dayOf(ws.weekStart) < row.thresholdFull`): `thr = dayOf(last) >= row.thresholdShort ? [{ ...NEXT.lighterThr }] : []`, and no `second`. Else the existing branches.
   - **Strides.** `strides: row && dayOf(last) < row.strides ? 0 : undefined` in the shape passed to `build()`.
   - **Tempo, cross:** unchanged (`v.lighter` already turns them off in recovery weeks).
4. After `aroundRace`, apply the day gates to every week when `row` is set:

   ```ts
   const items = gateAfterRace(seed.race ? aroundRace(built, seed.race.date, ws.weekStart) : built, rec, row);
   ```

   New function next to `aroundRace`:

   ```ts
   /**
    * The days after a race of 10 km+ (docs/decisions/fartlek-post-race-recovery.md): no running on days 1..restDays,
    * strides only from `strides`, threshold only from `thresholdShort` (moved later in the week when a later easy day can take it,
    * else run easy), a race-phase session only from `intervals`. Race day and the days before it are aroundRace's.
    */
   export function gateAfterRace(items: SuggestItem[], rec: RecoveryRace | null, row: RecoveryRow | null): SuggestItem[]
   ```

   Rules, item by item, with `d = daysBetween(rec.date, it.date)`; items with `d <= 0` pass untouched:
   - `d <= row.restDays` and `it.type !== "cross"`: drop it (the week lands under its target; that is intended).
   - `it.type === "threshold"` and `d < row.thresholdShort`: swap dates with the **latest** easy run in the same week whose `d >= row.thresholdShort`; with none, turn it into `{ date, type: "easy", km: it.km, durationMin: null, steps: null }` (the km stays so the week adds up; drop `blueprint`).
   - `it.blueprint` set and `d < row.intervals`: a long one becomes a plain long run (`steps: null`, km kept, no `blueprint`); a quality one becomes easy as above.
   - `it.type === "easy"` with strides (`stridesOf(it.steps)`) and `d < row.strides`: `steps: null`, km kept.
   - Return sorted by date.
5. After the week is built: `if (v.role === "recovery") recLongest = Math.max(recLongest, ...items.filter(isRun).map((it) => it.km));` The existing `longest`/`any` updates stay; together with the reset above they make the first build week step from the recovery weeks' longest run. (`recLongest` is only for the test's sake; drop it if unused.)

### Step 4: the search and the verdict

No change is needed, but check each with a test:

- `evaluateBlock`: `build` filters `role === "build"`, so recovery weeks are not the deepest week and don't trip the ramp flag. A block of only recovery weeks has `deepest: null` and state `ok`.
- `fitWeeks`: skips `v.lighter` weeks, recovery included.
- `scaleFor` and a goal: the scale only touches build weeks, so a goal is met by the build weeks after recovery; `shortBy` says when it can't be.
- `suggestBlock`'s first-week cap: only when week 0 is a build week.
- `deepen` and `taperFit`: pass `undefined` for weeks that aren't build or lighter, so recovery weeks are recomputed by `volumes()` the same way each time.
- `isLighter` returns false for a recovery week (roles decide); `PlannedWeek.lighter` is true (from `volumes`). Same split as a taper week today.

### Step 5: routes (`server/routes/blocks.ts`, `server/routes/blockplan.ts`)

1. **Find the race** (new, in `server/routes/blocks.ts` next to `longestRun`, exported so the week route can use it):

   ```ts
   /** The A or B race of 10 km+ whose recovery reaches into the weeks from `startWeek` (docs/decisions/fartlek-post-race-recovery.md). */
   export function recoveryRaceFor(ctx: Ctx, startWeek: ISODate): (RecoveryRace & { name: string }) | null
   ```

   - Candidates: races on the race list with `priority` A or B, `distanceKm >= RECOVERY.minKm`, `date >= addDays(startWeek, -RECOVERY.lookbackDays)` and `date < max(startWeek, ctx.today)`; plus race sessions (`type === "race"`, `status !== "dropped"`, `distanceKm >= RECOVERY.minKm`) in the same window whose date is not on the race list, as priority **B** (the race list is where A is set).
   - Keep those whose recovery still reaches the first week: `recoveryRoles(r, startWeek, 1)[0] === "recovery"` **or** a day gate is still closed on the week's last day (`daysBetween(r.date, addDays(startWeek, 6)) < row.intervals`). Of several, the one whose `addDays(date, row.intervals)` is latest.
   - `peakKm`: `Math.max(0, ...weeksBefore(ctx, weekStartOf(r.date, ctx.settings.weekStart), 6).map((w) => w.km)) || null`.
   - `longestRun` gets an optional `skip: ISODate[]` (activities and sessions on those days don't count).
2. **`blockSeedFor`**: new option `recovery?: RecoveryRace | null`. When set, put it on the seed and recompute `seed.longestKm = longestRun(ctx, addDays(startWeek, -NEXT.spikeDays), addDays(startWeek, -1), [recovery.date])`, so the race itself is not the long run the ladder and the spike rule step from.
3. **Roles.** New pure helper in `shared/blockplan.ts`:

   ```ts
   /** Recovery weeks first (a race week inside them stays a race week, a taper week becomes recovery); the rest as given, or build. */
   export function withRecovery(roles: WeekRole[] | null, rec: (WeekRole | null)[]): WeekRole[] | null {
     if (!rec.some(Boolean)) return roles;
     return rec.map((r, i) => (r && roles?.[i] !== "race" ? r : roles?.[i] ?? "build"));
   }
   ```

   With no roles given the weeks after recovery are all `build` and the block has **no lighter week** (the recovery weeks were the easy part). In `blockSeedFor`, run the existing tired-start rule only when `roles[0] === "build"` (it already checks), so a recovery first week is never turned lighter.
4. **`GET /plan/block-suggest`**:
   - Delete the `raceJustRun` call and its `afterRace` response.
   - `const rec = recoveryRaceFor(ctx, startWeek);` then `const recRoles = rec ? recoveryRoles(rec, startWeek, weeks) : [];` and pass `roles: withRecovery(rp?.roles ?? null, recRoles) ?? undefined` and `recovery: rec` to `blockSeedFor`.
   - The `rhythm=1:1` lighter weeks apply only when no recovery week is in the block (`!rp && !recRoles.some(Boolean)`).
   - Also set `recovery` on the seed when a race of 10 km+ is **inside** the planned weeks (race planning), with `peakKm: null`, so the race week's days after the race are gated too.
   - Add to the JSON: `recovery: rec ? { name: rec.name, date: rec.date, distanceKm: rec.distanceKm, priority: rec.priority, weeks: recRoles.filter(Boolean).length } : null`.
5. **`POST /plan/block-suggest`**: delete the `raceJustRun` 409. Add `"recovery"` to `ROLES`. When `b.blocks` is not sent and every role is `recovery`, write the block with `kind: "recovery"` (already a `BlockKind`), else `base`. `writeTargets`: a `recovery` week gets the note `RECOVERY_NOTE = "recovery"` (a new constant next to `TAPER_NOTE`).

7. **Re-plan** (`GET /blocks/:id/replan`):
   - `replanRace(race, startWeek, weeks)`: replace the `lighter` fill after the race week with `recoveryRoles({ date: race.date, distanceKm: race.distanceKm, priority, peakKm: null }, startWeek, weeks)` for the weeks after it, `build` where that gives null. ### Edge cases (checklist)

- **Race on any weekday.** The middle-day rule in `recoveryWeekOf` decides the volume week; the day gates are per day, so a Wednesday race and a Sunday race open threshold on the same day count.
- **Block created late.** Weeks already over are not planned, recovery weeks that are over are dropped by `recoveryRoles`, and a build week inside the gate window still has its gates (step 3.4).
- **Block starting in the running week** (from today): the race may be earlier the same week; `recoveryRaceFor` looks before `max(startWeek, today)`, and `recoveryWeekOf` never goes below 0.
- **Two races** (a half, then a 10K two weeks later): the one whose `intervals` gate ends last wins; only one race's recovery is planned.
- **A race ahead and a recovery behind** (a B race four weeks after a marathon): `withRecovery` keeps the race week as `race` and makes the recovery weeks first; a taper week inside recovery becomes recovery.
- **No history before the race** (`peakKm` null, no tolerance): the peak falls back to the weeks before the block; with nothing, `NEXT.startKm` via the lighter branch.
- **A race session without a race-list row** counts as B; a dropped race session counts as not run. A race-list row in the past counts as run (results don't change anything in v1).
- **Too few run days** for the share (the long-run cap keeps easy runs short): the week may land under its target. `writeTargets` writes the sum actually planned.
- **The runner switches a week's role** in the editor to or from `recovery`: without `seed.recovery` it behaves as a lighter week (70 % of the week before).
